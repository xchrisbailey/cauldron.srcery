Garlic butter pasta

Ingredients
200g spaghetti
3 tbsp butter
4 cloves garlic, minced
1/2 cup grated parmesan
salt and pepper

Method
1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
2. Melt the butter in a pan and cook the garlic for 1 minute.
3. Toss the pasta in the butter with a splash of pasta water.
4. Stir through the parmesan and serve.
