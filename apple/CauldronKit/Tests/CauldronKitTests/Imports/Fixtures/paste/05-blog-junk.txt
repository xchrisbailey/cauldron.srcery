The Best Crispy Roast Potatoes

Posted on March 3 by Anna | 14 comments

When I was a kid my grandmother roasted potatoes every Sunday and the whole house smelled of rosemary. I have been chasing that smell ever since, and after many failed trays I finally think I have got it.

Ingredients
- 1 kg floury potatoes, peeled
- 4 tbsp goose fat
- 2 sprigs rosemary
- 1 tsp flaky salt

Instructions
1. Parboil the potatoes for 10 minutes, then drain and shake them to roughen the edges.
2. Heat the fat in a roasting tin in a 220C oven.
3. Add the potatoes and rosemary and roast for 50 minutes, turning once.
