Chicken Curry

Ingredients
4 chicken thighs
1 onion, chopped
2 tbsp curry paste
1 tin coconut milk

Method
1. Brown the chicken thighs in a large pan
over a high heat, then set aside.
2. Cook the onion in the same pan until soft,
stir in the curry paste and fry for 1 minute.
3. Return the chicken, pour in the coconut milk
and simmer for 25 minutes.
