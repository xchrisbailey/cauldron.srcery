Lemon Drizzle Cake

Ingredients
225 g butter, softened
225 g caster sugar
4 eggs
225 g self-raising flour
zest of 2 lemons

Method
1. Heat the oven to 180C. Line a loaf tin with
baking paper.
2. Beat the butter and sugar together until pale
and fluffy, then beat in the eggs one at a
time.
3. Fold in the flour and the lemon zest, then
spoon into the tin and bake for 45 minutes.
