Fluffy pancakes 🥞 #pancakes #breakfast

Ingredients:
- 1 cup flour #baking
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 egg
- 3/4 cup milk

Method:
1. Whisk the dry ingredients together. #easy
2. Add the egg and milk and stir until just combined.
3. Cook spoonfuls in a buttered pan until golden on both sides.
