Sticky teriyaki salmon bowls 🍚🐟

Ingredients:
For the salmon:
- 2 salmon fillets
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 cup cooked rice

For the sauce:
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 clove garlic, grated

Method:
1. Mix the sauce ingredients in a small bowl.
2. Sear the salmon in the oil for 4 minutes per side.
3. Pour over the sauce and let it bubble until sticky.
4. Serve over the rice.
