Pumpkin Bread

Every fall my kitchen smells like cinnamon, and this loaf is the reason. I first made it for a school bake sale and never stopped.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) pumpkin puree
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 3/4 cups flour
  • 2 tsp pumpkin pie spice

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350 F and grease a loaf pan.
  2. Whisk the pumpkin, sugar, oil and eggs.
  3. Stir in the flour and spice, pour into the pan and bake for 60 minutes.
