Zucchini Fritters

Ingredients

  • 2 zucchini, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup flour

Method

  1. Squeeze the water out of the zucchini.
  2. Mix with the egg and flour.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in oil until crisp.
