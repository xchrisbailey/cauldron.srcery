Chicken Tikka Masala

Creamy, mild and ready in an hour.

What you'll need

  • 1 lb chicken thighs, cubed
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp tikka masala paste
  • 1 can (14 oz) tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup cream

Directions

  1. Marinate the chicken in the yogurt and half the paste for 30 minutes.
  2. Sear the chicken in a hot pan.
  3. Add the tomato sauce, remaining paste and cream and simmer for 15 minutes.
