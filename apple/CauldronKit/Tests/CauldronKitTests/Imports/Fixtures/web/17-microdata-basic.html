Classic Meatloaf

Ruth Adler

A weeknight meatloaf with a sticky glaze.

6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup ketchup

Method

  1. Mix the beef, breadcrumbs and eggs.
  2. Shape into a loaf and brush with ketchup.
  3. Bake at 350 F for 1 hour.
