Coconut Curry Lentils

Get new recipes in your inbox

Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
Buy our spice kit

Directions

  1. Simmer the lentils in 2 cups water for 15 minutes.
  2. Stir in the coconut milk and curry powder and heat through.
