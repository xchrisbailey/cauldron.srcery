# Cauldron

Cauldron keeps recipes and the unfinished work of bringing them into a personal recipe box.

## Language

**Shared draft**:
An unfinished recipe sent to Cauldron from another app, including any corrections made before it reaches the full editor. It remains unfinished until the cook saves it as a recipe or explicitly discards it.
_Avoid_: Saved recipe, finished import

**Saved recipe**:
A recipe the cook has chosen to keep in the recipe box. Keeping an unfinished shared draft for later does not save it as a recipe.
_Avoid_: Shared draft
