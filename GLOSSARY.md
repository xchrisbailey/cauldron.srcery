# Cauldron

Cauldron keeps recipes and the unfinished work of bringing them into a personal recipe box.

## Language

**Shared draft**:
An unfinished recipe sent to Cauldron from another app, including any corrections made before it reaches the full editor. It remains unfinished until the cook saves it as a recipe or explicitly discards it.
_Avoid_: Saved recipe, finished import

**Saved recipe**:
A recipe the cook has chosen to keep in the recipe box. Keeping an unfinished shared draft for later does not save it as a recipe.
_Avoid_: Shared draft

**Planned batch**:
A quantity of one recipe intended for a particular week, whose servings can be assigned to days. Separate additions of the same recipe are separate planned batches; planning a batch does not mean it has been cooked.
_Avoid_: Leftovers, cooking history

**Unassigned servings**:
The servings of a planned batch that have not yet been assigned to a day. They describe unfinished scheduling, not food left uneaten.
_Avoid_: Leftover servings, uneaten servings

**Close match**:
A search result that needed forgiving: a typo, another name for a word typed, or one missing word. Close matches are listed after the exact matches, under their own heading, each with a plain line saying what it matched. A recipe that matches every word as typed is an exact match, never a close one.
_Avoid_: Fuzzy match, suggestion, did you mean
